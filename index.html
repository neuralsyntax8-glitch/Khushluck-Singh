About

The NeuralSyntax Story

I started coding in 2023 with Python. I was 13, in Delhi, and like most students, I had no idea where to begin. Tutorials were either too expensive or too shallow — so I decided to build my own path.

By 2024, I had built my first real projects. By 2025, I launched NeuralSyntax — a YouTube channel and learning initiative. The goal: make AI and programming education accessible, practical, and free for Indian students.

I don't just consume content. I build. Over three years, I have built student performance predictors, AI study planners, automation scripts, and more. Every project is published, every lesson is shared on YouTube.

I'm not a student showing homework. I'm a young AI builder and tech educator — building real tools, earning real certifications, and helping students learn through technology.
